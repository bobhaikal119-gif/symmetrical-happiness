## Final settled behavior

- Liquid work: MAX_ACTIVE_PER_STEP2048; hydraulic traversal MAX_PRESSURE_CELLS128 per search and MAX_PRESSURE_WORK_PER_STEP8192 per tick. Connected saturated-path search handles bent/roofed conduits with conservative donor/receiver transfer.
- sample_submersion is point penetration below the cell waterline (0 above surface), not body fraction. Swimming integrates head-to-feet vertical overlap into normalized0..1 body coverage; head underwater is a separate boolean.
- LiquidRenderer is unshaded/translucent for readable cyan volumes/foam in dark caves; particle cap<=128, maximum14 per burst and finite lifetime.
- LiquidRuntimeCoordinator child monitors typed BlastDoor closure and supplies blocked cells before liquid ticks. Terrain changes directly wake the solver; displaced fluid is serialized reserve until adjacent capacity returns.
- SaveManager is explicit liquid-only state, with seed/dimensions/SHA256 terrain identity guard and no automatic load. Profile tab emits save/load requests.
- Deferred AimCrosshair activity callbacks guard detached/queued scenes, so reset never queries a null viewport.
